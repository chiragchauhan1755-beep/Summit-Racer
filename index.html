1. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled in connection with the mobile application Summit Racer Car (Android package ID: [YOUR_ANDROID_PACKAGE_ID]), developed and offered by The Data Sequence ("we", "us", or "our").

We are committed to transparency regarding privacy practices when you download, install, or play Summit Racer Car on your mobile device.

2. Introduction

Summit Racer Car is a mobile hill-climbing racing game for Android devices, distributed through the Google Play Store. Players drive vehicles across varied terrain, collect in-game items, upgrade vehicles, and progress through stages and challenges.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when someone uses the App. It covers the analytics and potential advertising technologies used by or integrated into the App.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using Summit Racer Car. By downloading, accessing, or playing Summit Racer Car, you acknowledge the privacy practices described in this document. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not install or use the App.

3. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with the App, your device settings, and integrated third-party services, certain technical, usage, and diagnostic information may be processed automatically.

Potential categories of information that may be processed include:

  • Device Information: Device model, manufacturer, screen resolution, hardware properties, and system preferences.
  • Operating System Information: OS type (Android) and OS version.
  • App Information: App version, build number, package identifier, and installation source.
  • Language & Region: System language preferences and country/regional settings.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse/approximate location derived from IP address or network carrier information, where provided by third-party services.
  • IP Address & Network Info: Internet Protocol address and general connection type (e.g., Wi-Fi, cellular network).
  • Advertising Identifiers: Mobile advertising IDs such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID), subject to device configuration and consent.
  • App Usage & Gameplay Interactions: Game sessions, races started or completed, stages selected, distance records, vehicle selections and upgrades, collectible pickups, missions, rewards, and general feature interactions, where these events are recorded.
  • Diagnostic Information: Crash reports, system performance metrics, and error logs where applicable.
Important Distinction: Not all categories of information listed above are stored or personally processed directly by the developer. A distinction is made between information collected directly by the App and information that may be collected automatically by integrated third-party SDKs and service providers (such as Firebase Analytics or advertising networks).

4. Information Collected Automatically

When you launch and play Summit Racer Car, integrated software development kits (SDKs) and system services may process technical data automatically to deliver gameplay features, support advertising or analytics, and assess performance.

This automated processing occurs in the background when the application runs on your Android device and does not require explicit manual input from you during gameplay.

5. Device and Technical Information

Technical and device information may be processed for the following operational purposes:

  • To ensure proper operation and core functionality of the game.
  • To maintain device and software compatibility across different Android hardware.
  • To troubleshoot technical glitches, application crashes, and performance issues.
  • To improve app optimization and user experience.
  • To protect the game and service infrastructure against abuse, fraud, or security threats.

We do not collect or store hardware serial numbers or persistent un-resettable hardware identifiers unless explicitly disclosed or required for technical service operations.

6. Gameplay and Usage Information

The App may process gameplay metrics and usage interactions for analytics and game design improvement purposes.

The App may record gameplay events for analytics purposes, such as:

  • Game session initiation, duration, and app launch frequency.
  • Race and stage starts, race completions, failed runs, and race restarts.
  • Use of in-game features, such as selecting vehicles or stages, upgrading vehicles, collecting coins or fuel, using boosts, and claiming rewards.
  • Gameplay progression, achievements, and milestone events.
  • General navigation within app menus and UI screens.

These events help us understand race and stage difficulty, vehicle balance, which features players use, and where improvements may be needed.

7. Analytics Information

Analytics information refers to statistical data collected to analyze how Summit Racer Car is downloaded, launched, and played.

Analytics data is typically evaluated in an aggregated or pseudonymized form to assess general usage trends rather than identifying individual human beings.

8. Firebase Analytics

Where enabled in the app, Google Firebase Analytics (also known as Google Analytics for Firebase) may be used to understand app usage, measure player engagement, and improve game stability and design.

Firebase Analytics is an analytics service provided by Google LLC (or Google Ireland Limited, depending on your region). Through Firebase Analytics, the following types of data may be automatically processed:

  • App interactions and custom gameplay events.
  • Session counts, durations, and retention metrics.
  • Device brand, model, operating system version, and system language.
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address).
  • App version and build updates.
  • Diagnostic and error data related to app sessions.

The developer does not personally receive or store raw individual user profiles collected by Firebase Analytics; rather, Google processes this data to provide aggregate reporting and dashboard insights to the developer.

For details on how Google processes data through Firebase, please consult the official Google privacy documentation:
https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

12. Advertising Identifiers

Mobile advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID on Android devices) are unique, user-resettable string identifiers provided by the mobile operating system.

Advertising services may use mobile advertising identifiers to:

  • Measure advertising campaign effectiveness and viewability.
  • Limit ad repetition and detect fraudulent traffic.

You can control, reset, or opt out of personalized advertising identifiers at any time through your Android device settings or through regional consent banners presented within the App where applicable.
